# FlipFinder roadmap

## V1 — current release

iPhone opportunity intelligence for OLX Poland: browser collection, persistent market benchmarks, AI risk signals, deterministic resale, scoring, Telegram reports, and local Web Admin.

## V2 direction

- Configurable Product Profiles.
- Generic Product Identity and category-specific attributes.
- Multiple simultaneous product profiles.
- Multi-category market benchmarks.

## Later

- More marketplace adapters, including supported Allegro flows.
- Cross-market comparison and deduplication.
- Rule builder for product-specific operating policies.

## Future possibilities

- Optional hosted/cloud monitoring.
- Additional notification channels.
- Advanced analytics.

No dates are committed. Marketplace compliance and local-user control remain core constraints.
